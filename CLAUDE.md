## Project
This is the website for **Digital Ops Systems**, founded by Lee Scott.

## Domain
`digitalopsystems.com` (double s — "ops" + "systems"). Any reference to `digitalopsistems.com` or `digitalopssystems.com` is a bug; correct it on sight.

## Business Positioning (RATIFIED 2026-06-06)

DOPS helps people who have the **will to achieve but are blocked only by a knowledge gap in tech and AI** — and proves it works before asking them to fully pay.

The offer narrows to four things in order: (1) lean Websites, (2) SEO, (3) Google EEAT, (4) CRM. We are not a broad automation consultancy. We sell **help and outcomes**, not "AI agents."

## Three Tiers (public pricing — current as of 2026-06-06)

- **Foundation** — $1,800 setup + $450/mo retainer
- **Growth** — $2,800 setup + $850/mo retainer *(most popular — default tier)*
- **Authority** — $3,500 setup + $1,200/mo retainer
- Entry product: **Business Operations Assessment $497** (carried from v1, still active)

Plus an **optional proof-first option** (`"ask us about our prove-it-first option"`) — case-by-case, no binding terms published until Louisiana attorney clears the contract.

## Audience

Not a demographic — a **condition**: *willing but underequipped.* Solo entrepreneurs who think tech/AI is too hard. SMBs needing a real website + SEO + CRM. The Black community + youth. Music industry + creator businesses. Regional anchor: Louisiana + Texas. Geography-agnostic for higher tiers but the regional SEO investment is load-bearing — do not strip it.

## Voice (RATIFIED — helper-not-hype)

The guy who's been where you are and learned the tool fast so he could bring you with him.
- **Encouraging, never intimidating.** "It's not as hard as you think."
- **Operator, not academic.** Plain language, real stakes.
- **Invitational close.** "Give us a chance."
- **Grounded and real, never hype.** No "fire your team," no magic bullets, no replaced-my-team energy.
- Site voice: **"we"** (consultancy framing). Footer brand h3 reads **Lee Scott** (founder personal). Do not switch site voice from "we" to "I."

## The 4-beat content arc (run on EVERY page)

Every page should move the visitor through:
1. **Recognized** — "this person gets me, he's been where I am"
2. **It's doable** — "this is actually doable for me"
3. **Move now** — "this is the railroad moment"
4. **Trust** — "these people are grounded and real, not hype"

## Mission

**Help 100 business owners build something real.** This is the north star. Aimed at the person who thinks tech/AI is too hard for them. Lives as a section on the homepage (`#mission` anchor).

## Faith / Hosea 4:6 = PRIVATE ENGINE

Expressed through HOW PEOPLE ARE TREATED. **Never overt iconography, Scripture, or church imagery in public output.**

## Design Rules

- Simple HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks unless explicitly asked.
- One consistent header, nav, footer, spacing, visual style across all pages.
- Colors: navy `#0D1B2A`, gold `#C9A84C`, white.
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts).
- Modern, clear, business-focused, reassuring.
- Visual signature (per `knowledge/zaza/wiki/visual-brand.md`): **clean, professional, credible — warmed by genuine human energy.** Hold both — never drift fully corporate, never drift fully casual.

## Messaging Rules

- **Outcomes first, mechanisms second.** What it does for the business comes before how it works.
- **Lead with recognition + doability.** Don't lead with fear. Lead with "you can do this; we'll show you how."
- **Two clear entry points.** Pricing page (Foundation/Growth/Authority) and a free strategy call.
- **No "AI Workers" / "AI Automation Systems" hero language.** Those describe what's *included* in packages; they are not the brand frame.
- **Mention proof-first lightly only** — never publish binding terms until attorney clears.

## What NOT to do

- Do not change the brand name, domain, or color system.
- Do not remove Blog or Projects from the navigation.
- Do not drop the regional/local SEO investment (LocalBusiness schema, Baton Rouge address, Louisiana + Texas service areas, sitemap, robots.txt, Google site verification meta).
- Do not switch the site voice from "we" to "I" (founder personal in footer h3 only).
- Do not add a chat widget, popup, or newsletter modal.
- Do not add a team page (still a single-author practice).
- Do not run a visual redesign — the existing design system holds up; we update *copy* to match the 2026-06-06 pivot, not visuals.
- Do not reintroduce "AI Automation Systems" / "Premium tier custom engagements" framing on the homepage hero or pricing.
- Do not publish binding proof-first terms (window, deposit, trigger) until Louisiana attorney clearance is logged.
- Do not introduce religious or political framing in public copy — faith is PRIVATE engine, expressed through tone.
- Do not break the LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema. Validate after any homepage edit.
