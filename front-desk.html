Coming Soon · Working name "Front Desk"

Your Digital Front Desk

Front Desk answers, books, and never misses a customer.

You can't answer the phone mid-haircut or mid-job — and every missed call is a customer who called someone else. Front Desk is an always-on AI that greets people, answers their questions, books the appointment, and keeps the conversation going across your channels — then drops it all neatly into your CRM. Like a receptionist who works 24/7 and never forgets a name.

What Front Desk will do

💬 Answer every inquiry

  • Replies to calls, texts, and messages instantly, day or night.
  • Answers the common questions — hours, pricing, services, location.
  • Sounds like your business, not a robot.

📅 Book the appointment

  • Offers open times and books the customer right into your calendar.
  • Sends confirmations and reminders so they actually show up.
  • Handles reschedules without you lifting a finger.

🧾 Capture every lead

  • No inquiry slips through — even the after-hours ones.
  • Every conversation is logged straight into your CRM.
  • You wake up to a tidy list, not a missed-call voicemail pile.

🤝 Know when to hand off

  • Flags anything that needs a real person and passes it to you.
  • You stay in control of anything sensitive or high-value.
  • A human is always one tap away for the customer.

Who it's for

Appointment-based businesses that lose customers to missed calls and slow replies — barbershops, salons, nail studios, and independent pros who can't stop mid-service to answer the phone. If you've ever thought "I need a receptionist but can't justify hiring one," Front Desk is built for you.
