The New-Customer Engine

Scout finds your next customers — so you don't have to.

You're great at the work. Prospecting is the part nobody has time for. Scout does it quietly in the background: it finds local businesses and people who fit what you offer, and hands them to you — organized and ready — so a steady trickle of new leads shows up without you cold-calling all day.

What Scout will do

🔎 Find the right prospects

  • Surfaces local businesses and people who match your ideal customer.
  • Filters out the noise so you only see leads worth your time.
  • Keeps a fresh list coming — not a one-time dump.

📋 Hand them over ready

  • Each prospect arrives organized, with the context you need.
  • Flows straight into your CRM so nothing gets lost.
  • You approve every outreach — it never spams on your behalf.

✍️ Draft the first hello

  • Suggests a friendly, on-brand opening message.
  • Written to start a conversation, never to hard-sell.
  • You send when you're ready — a human is always in the loop.

📈 Keep the pipeline full

  • A predictable flow of new opportunities every week.
  • Less feast-or-famine, more steady growth.
  • Built to run whether or not you have a free minute.

Who it's for

Owners who are excellent at their craft but hate chasing leads — barbers, stylists, nail techs, and real estate agents building a book of business, plus any local business that wants new customers without living on the phone. If prospecting is the thing that always slips, Scout is built for you.
