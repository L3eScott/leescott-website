Instagram is where your clients hang out. Google is where new clients find you. If your shop isn't on Google the right way, you're losing customers to the shop two blocks over — and they probably aren't even better than you.

What's working against you right now

The four things almost every shop is missing.

We've looked at a lot of shop sites. The same four gaps show up almost every time — not because owners don't care, but because nobody showed them this part.

1. Your Google Business Profile is half-claimed.

Missing services, missing prices, missing photos updated in the last six months. The version of your shop Google shows is not the version of your shop you actually run.

2. You have no real website — just a Linktree.

A Linktree doesn't rank. A Booksy page doesn't rank. Google needs a place that actually describes who you are, what you do, and where you do it — or you're invisible to "fade near me" searches.

3. The reviews live on Google, not on your site.

200+ Instagram followers say you're the best. Google doesn't know. The trust signal stays trapped on one platform.

4. Bookings live in your DMs.

Booksy or Square is fine for the booking itself. But every appointment that started as a DM is a lead that never made it into a system — so the follow-up, the rebook reminder, the birthday text never happens.
