You're in the bay, not in front of a website. Meanwhile somebody two streets over with worse work but better Google placement gets called first when somebody's check engine light comes on. That's fixable.

What's working against you right now

Four gaps almost every shop has.

We've audited a lot of independent shop sites. The same four gaps show up almost every time — and any one of them is enough to keep you off the local pack.

1. Your Google Business Profile is one category deep.

Most shops list "Auto Repair Shop" and stop. Google has separate categories for "Brake Shop," "Tire Shop," "Oil Change Service," "Transmission Shop" — and you can stack them. Most shops don't, so the "brake repair near me" search goes to the shop that did.

2. No service-area or service-specific pages.

A site with one "Services" page can't rank for "brake repair [your suburb]" or "AC repair [your city]." You need a page per service in each area you actually serve. Most shops don't have any.

3. Zero schema markup.

Google reads structured data to understand who you are, what services you offer, and what your hours and reviews look like. Most independent sites send none of it. The Wix template doesn't put it there.

4. Your reviews live on Google. Nowhere else.

Customers say great things on your GBP. Your website doesn't show them. The trust signal that should be on every service page is trapped on one platform.
