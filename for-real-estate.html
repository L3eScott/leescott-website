What's working against you right now

Four reasons buyers find everyone but you.

You know your market cold. But the way agents get found online has four gaps that send your next client to someone with a worse pitch and a better page.

1. You live inside your brokerage's website.

A profile page on the brokerage site isn't you — it's a template every agent at the office shares, and it ranks for the brokerage, not for your name. When a past client refers you, there's nowhere strong to land.

2. You're fighting for the one term you can't win.

Everyone chases "[city] real estate agent" — against Zillow, Realtor.com, and every brokerage in town. Meanwhile the winnable searches — the specific neighborhoods you actually work — sit wide open.

3. Your reviews are trapped on Zillow and Google.

Years of five-star closings, and the proof lives on a platform that shows your competitors right next to you. On your own site, your reviews are the first thing a new client sees — with no one else in frame.

4. Leads live in your phone, not a system.

A missed call or a form that goes to an inbox you check twice a day is a buyer who already messaged two other agents. Capture and instant follow-up are the difference between a lead and a closing.
